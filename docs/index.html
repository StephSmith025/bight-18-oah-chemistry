2-5 Hovmollers

These Hovmöller diagrams summarize latitude–time patterns in oceanographic conditions across the Southern California Bight. Interpolated surfaces were generated from individual CTD observations using a thin-plate spline and are intended to illustrate regional-scale patterns rather than conditions at unsampled locations. Faceting is used where appropriate to reduce interpolation across distinct monitoring regions. Hovmöllers are provided as the entire dataset in a single figure for select parameters. A band of extended distance between OCSAN and San Diego plots that was bordered on either side only by OA Monitoring stations was causing anomalous interpolation in this region. Therefore I decided to facet the hovmollers into separate cross-shore categories. Where faceted by distance category; categories are currently out of order. I suggest removing the nearshore category entirely for lack of meaningful data; but have left it in for now for your review. Where faceted by monitoring category (regular water quality vs OA monitoring stations) the NOAA ONI is plotted across the top. ONI can be added to any figure.
